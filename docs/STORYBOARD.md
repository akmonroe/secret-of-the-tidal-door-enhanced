# Secret of the Tidal Door — Full Storyboard

**Target length:** 12 main levels (expandable to 15)  
**Audience:** Grade school & middle school  
**View:** Top-down adventure (Sneaky Sasquatch style)  
**Prize:** The **Rainbow Coral** — a living coral that shines every color of the sea  
**Core loop each level:** Explore a **maze-like region** → avoid/route past **obstacles & creatures** → collect a **Clue Fragment** → exit opens toward the next region  

---

## Overall story

An old legend says the **Rainbow Coral** only appears to explorers who prove they can respect every region of the ocean. It does not sit in one obvious place — its resting ground is revealed only when **twelve clue fragments** are gathered from twelve different seas.

Your explorer (girl or boy) starts on a familiar beach, finds a secret house and scuba gear, then travels farther than any local map shows: reefs, shipwrecks, kelp forests, ice shelves, volcanic vents, and a final spiral lagoon where the clues become a complete map.

**Tone:** Wonder, mild tension, kid-safe thrills. Failures restart the level (or a nearby checkpoint), never permanent loss of collected clues once a level is cleared.

---

## Clue system (meta-puzzle)

| | |
|--|--|
| **What you find** | A glowing **Clue Shell** / tablet / bottle at the end of each level’s maze |
| **What it gives** | One fragment of the final riddle + a stamp on the **Ocean Journal** |
| **HUD** | Journal icon shows `Clues: N / 12` |
| **Final level** | All 12 fragments rearrange into: *“Where seven colors meet the quiet eye of the tide”* → **Eye of the Lagoon** (Level 12) |

### The twelve fragments (in order found)

1. **SUN** — “Begin where light first touches sand.”  
2. **SALT** — “Cross the house that walks on water.”  
3. **BREATH** — “Wear the tank that drinks the deep.”  
4. **GOLD** — “Past the coins the wreck still keeps.”  
5. **GREEN** — “Through forests that never need the sky.”  
6. **WHITE** — “Where ice remembers winter’s bite.”  
7. **BLACK** — “Follow warmth where night is bright.” *(vents / glow)*  
8. **SILVER** — “Race the fish that flash like knives.”  
9. **PURPLE** — “Climb the stairs the coral builds.”  
10. **STORM** — “Hold steady when the sky falls in.” *(surface squall / churn)*  
11. **MIRROR** — “Find the cave that copies you.”  
12. **RAINBOW** — “All colors join where currents rest.” → reveals prize room  

Fragments 1–11 are **collected at level ends**. Fragment 12 is revealed when entering the prize chamber (Level 12 climax), not as a separate fetch.

## Pacing for kids

| Block | Levels | Play time (est.) | Feel |
|-------|--------|------------------|------|
| **Act I — Threshold** | 1–3 | 8–15 min | Learn move, dodge, swim, scuba |
| **Act II — Wide Ocean** | 4–8 | 20–35 min | New biomes, denser mazes |
| **Act III — Deep & Trial** | 9–11 | 15–25 min | Harder mazes, multi-stage clues |
| **Finale** | 12 | 5–10 min | Combine clues → Rainbow Coral |
| **Optional (13–15)** | bonus | — | Hard remixes / new game+ |

**Total main path:** ~50–80 minutes with exploring (or shorter for skilled players).

## Level-by-level storyboard

Legend for **Maze type**:
- **Open maze** — wide space, soft walls (rocks, sandbars), free pathfinding  
- **Corridor maze** — walls force routes (house, wreck, ice, caves)  
- **Living maze** — barriers move (kelp, fish schools, current gates)  
- **Hybrid** — fixed walls + moving hazards

## Obstacle & maze toolkit (reuse across levels)

Build a shared “maze kit” so 12 levels stay implementable:

| Piece | Behavior | Use in |
|-------|----------|--------|
| **Solid wall tiles** | Collision | All corridor levels |
| **Rock / coral clusters** | Soft open-maze blockers | L1, L3, L9, L12 |
| **Furniture / crates** | Small colliders | L2, L4 |
| **One-way current** | Constant velocity zone | L3, L8, L14 |
| **Timed gate** | Opens on interval | L4, L8, L10 |
| **Switch → door** | Pressure or touch plate | L4, L9, L11 |
| **Key pickup** | 1–3 keys per level | L9, L11 |
| **Moving barrier** | Sliding wall / kelp gap | L5, L6, L10 |
| **Hazard floor (pulse)** | Damage on red phase | L7 |
| **Push zone** | Wave / whirlpool | L8, L10 |
| **Checkpoint shell** | Respawn mid-level | L6–L12 |
| **Clue pedestal** | Level complete trigger | All |

**Creatures (roster):** shark, jelly, ray, pelican, gull *(surface)*, eel, crab, sea lion, seal, angler, triggerfish, crystal jelly, starfish guardian.

## Design principles (don’t break these)

1. **Each level looks different** — palette, hazards, maze verb.  
2. **Each level ends with a clue**, not a random dead end.  
3. **Mazes first, combat second** — dodge is for routing, not arena fights.  
4. **2–9 minutes per level** for kids; checkpoints from L6 onward.  
5. **Prize is only at the end** — earlier “treasure” is always a clue or tool.  
6. **No scare horror** — dark levels stay wondrous, not nightmare.

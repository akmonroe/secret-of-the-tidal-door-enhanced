# Grok Imagine prompts — Enhanced Tidal Door

**Style contract (repeat on every prompt):**  
Soft photorealism / nature-documentary materials, friendly saturated-but-natural colors for kids ~age 10. Soft daylight, readable shapes (Pixar-clear silhouettes). Never horror, gore, nightmare, or sexual content. Anglers/sharks impressive but toy-safe.

**Sprites:** Isolated subject, flat solid bright magenta `#FF00FF` background, no cast shadow on ground, clean silhouette, high-angle 3/4 or top-down for Switch-style camera.

**Tiles:** Seamless tileable 1:1 square, no logos/text, no unique landmarks that break tiling, non-directional lighting when possible.

---
